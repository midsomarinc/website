FAQ

How do I get started with Midsomar?

Install the CLI, point it at a repository, and connect the model of your choice. Midsomar indexes your codebase locally so it can reason about your architecture from the first session.

What kinds of tasks can Midsomar handle?

Midsomar excels at both routine development tasks like bug fixes and testing, as well as transformative work like refactors and feature implementation that require deep codebase understanding.

How does Midsomar work with my existing tools?

Midsomar plugs into your terminal and IDE, so engineers can hand it work from wherever they already are without changing their workflow.

Is Midsomar secure?

Midsomar deploys inside your own environment. Your code and prompts never leave your infrastructure unless you explicitly route them to an external model provider.

Can I bring my own model?

Yes. Midsomar is model-agnostic — connect a self-hosted model, a provider API, or switch between them per project without changing your workflow.

How much does Midsomar cost?

Individuals can start on usage-based API pricing. Teams and companies get custom pricing based on seats and deployment footprint — reach out and we'll walk you through it.

Does Midsomar work with self-hosted deployments?

Yes. Midsomar can run entirely inside your own VPC or on-prem environment, giving your security team full control over where code and data are processed.
